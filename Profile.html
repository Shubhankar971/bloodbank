About BloodBank

The blood bank department is a part of Dept. of Hematological Medicine which
also has a complementary Hematology lab and both units are under Head of Hematological Medicine.
This synergy facilitates quick diagnosis in bleeding emergencies and facilitates the prompt
issue of the necessary component therapy. The Dept. of Hematology is a post-graduate training centre
recognized by the MUHS (Maharashtra University of Health Sciences).
